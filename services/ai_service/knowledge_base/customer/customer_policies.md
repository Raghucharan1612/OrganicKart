# OrganicKart Customer Policies & Delivery Information

## OrganicKart Delivery Policy and Hours

OrganicKart quick-commerce delivery operates 7 days a week from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
Orders are delivered within 10 minutes using micro-fulfillment dark stores located close to customers.
Dedicated customer support is reachable at 1800-ORGANIC or via in-app notifications.

## Freshness & Quality Guarantee Policy

If any delivered organic produce does not meet freshness or quality standards, customers can initiate a return or replacement request immediately through the app.

## Cancellation & Refund Policy

Orders can be cancelled prior to dispatch. Refunds for prepaid orders are processed back to the original payment source within 3-5 business days.
