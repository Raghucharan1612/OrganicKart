# Customer Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What makes OrganicKart produce different from regular market produce?
OrganicKart produce is grown without synthetic chemical pesticides, artificial fertilizers, GMOs, or growth hormones. All items are sourced directly from certified organic farms and local growers.

## How does 10-Minute Delivery work?
OrganicKart operates micro-fulfillment dark stores situated close to customer delivery locations. When an order is placed, items are picked and packed immediately and dispatched via dedicated delivery partners.

## How do I verify if a product is certified organic?
Products on OrganicKart display certification status badges (e.g. APPROVED by OrganicKart quality auditors). Farmers and vendors must upload valid organic certification documentation before their products are approved for sale.

## Can I order products by weight?
Yes! OrganicKart supports weight-based pricing for produce like apples, mangoes, potatoes, and spices (e.g. per 100g, 250g, 500g, or 1kg).

## What payment methods are supported?
OrganicKart supports secure online digital payments via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, and Wallets).
