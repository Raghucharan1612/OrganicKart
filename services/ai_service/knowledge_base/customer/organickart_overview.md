# OrganicKart Overview

OrganicKart is India's premier quick-commerce organic marketplace. We deliver 100% certified organic fruits, vegetables, pure A2 dairy, cold-pressed oils, unpolished grains, and daily essentials straight from certified organic growers to customer kitchens in 10 minutes.

## Mission
Our mission is to make authentic, lab-certified organic food accessible, affordable, and delivered at lightning speed while empowering local organic farmers with direct marketplace access.

## Core Values
- 100% Lab & Admin Certified Organic: Every item listed undergoes strict quality and certification verification.
- Direct Grower Pricing: We eliminate unnecessary middlemen to give customers fair prices and farmers better earnings.
- Express 10-Minute Delivery: Temperature-controlled quick commerce fulfillment ensuring peak freshness.
- Transparency & Traceability: Customers can inspect grower details and certification credentials for listed produce.
