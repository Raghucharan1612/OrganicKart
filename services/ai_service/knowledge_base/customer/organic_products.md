# Organic Products Guide

## Types of Organic Products Available on OrganicKart

OrganicKart offers a wide variety of certified organic product types across multiple categories.
Customers can browse and order the following types of organic products:

- **Fruits**: Shimla & Kashmiri Apples, Alphonso & Kesar Mangoes, Papayas, Watermelons, Pineapples.
- **Vegetables**: Organic Tomatoes, Potatoes, Onions, Broccoli, Spinach, Carrots, Capsicum.
- **Pure A2 Dairy**: Fresh A2 Cow Milk, Pure Desi Ghee, Farm Paneer, Fresh Curd.
- **Cold-Pressed Oils**: Cold-Pressed Mustard Oil, Sesame Oil, Groundnut Oil, Extra Virgin Coconut Oil.
- **Grains & Pulses**: Unpolished Toor Dal, Moong Dal, Brown Rice, Organic Wheat Atta.

## Benefits of Organic Fruits & Vegetables

- Higher Nutrient Content: Organic fruits and vegetables often contain higher levels of antioxidants, vitamin C, and essential minerals like iron, magnesium, and chromium.
- Zero Harmful Pesticide Residue: Protects long-term health by eliminating intake of toxic agricultural chemicals.
- Richer Natural Taste: Grown in nutrient-dense organic soil, natural flavors and aromas are preserved.
- Environmentally Sustainable: Organic farming protects soil health, reduces water pollution, and promotes biodiversity.
